How a round plays

  1. Face-off. One player from each team answers. Tap their answers on the board, then pick which team won — they choose to play or pass.
  2. Play. The team in control keeps guessing. Tap correct answers to reveal them; press X for a strike.
  3. Steal. After the last strike, the other team gets one guess. Tap it if it's right, or hit “Steal failed”.
  4. End of round. The pot (× the round multiplier) goes to the winner. Reveal the rest and move on.

Tip: open Board display from the host menu to put a clean, controls-free board on a projector while you run the game from your laptop or phone.
